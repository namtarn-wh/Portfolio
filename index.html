About

I liken myself to a half-full cup of water — always open to learning and embracing new things.

My name is Nattaparee Whan-ying — Namtarn is my preferred name. Design has been part of my life since I was young, and I've only fallen more in love with it since; I believe it's an essential element across the board, in how a brand looks, feels, and is understood.

I currently work as a Graphic Designer on the Grab Thumbs Up team, creating and supporting visual assets that stay consistent with Grab's brand CI while bringing a more polished, elevated, and engaging look to each campaign. My work spans campaign artwork, social content, key visuals, and merchant-focused creative across food and lifestyle projects, alongside independent design work for clients like VIBES LAB and other food-and-beverage and hospitality brands. I studied Communication Design at Mahidol University International College, with a year abroad in Belgium studying fashion and design.

Cheerful, dedicated, and creative — three words I'd use for myself, and the same ones I try to bring to a brief.
